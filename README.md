**Meridian Bay** — an original sun-soaked open-world crime game built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty, $1,666 in USDC.

Kai Mercer, a driver-for-hire, is pulled into a turf war between the Harbor Kings and the Vesper Syndicate across five story missions: a delivery run, a car theft, a police getaway, a port shootout, and a final choice of sides. Explore a coastal 3D city on foot and by car — traffic, pedestrians, stealable cars, a 1–5 star wanted system with chasing police and roadblocks, pistol and melee combat, a live minimap, synthesized sound and music, and localStorage saves. All names, characters, art, and music are original.

## Run it

```sh
npx serve game
# or: python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory game
# then open http://localhost:8080
```

Controls: WASD move/drive · mouse orbit camera · Shift sprint · E enter/exit car · Space handbrake · LMB shoot · Q punch · R reload · F headlights · M map · Esc pause.
